Community Approach

A safe space is something we create together.

Young Black Creatives is rooted in trust, belonging and children’s right to shape their own creative experience.

A trusted third space

A place to be heard, feel at home and try.

Children need room to play, ask questions, make mistakes and discover who they are.

The studio offers a welcoming space close to home, where children can be themselves and express ideas through many forms. Relationships grow over time; listening, shared agreements and clear safety boundaries help children feel respected and able to take part.

Parents and caregivers are part of the circle of care around the programme. Communication, trust and respect for children’s privacy matter.

How we work

Support without taking over.

Adults provide materials, guidance when it is useful, and the boundaries that help keep the space safe. Children are trusted to contribute ideas, choose what to explore, and learn by doing.

Belonging

Agency

Care in practice

Listening, sharing materials and solving problems together turn care into a daily practice.

Learning as a community

Children grow things together.

One recent vertical garden project took six hours of shared focus and effort. Children planned, built, tested and cared for the garden together—learning patience and problem-solving along the way.
