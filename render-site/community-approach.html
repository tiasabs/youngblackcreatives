Community Approach

A safe space is something we create together.

Young Black Creatives is rooted in trust, belonging and children’s right to shape their own creative experience.

A welcoming studio

A place to make and be yourself.

Children need room to play, ask questions, make mistakes and discover who they are.

At open studio, adults set out materials and give children room to decide what to make. They test ideas, change direction and work out problems together. For guided sessions, visiting artists can teach a specific skill. Adults are there to help keep the studio safe.

Families can get in touch to learn how the programme works and what children are exploring.

How we work

Support without taking over.

Adults provide the materials and set clear safety boundaries. Children choose what to explore, share ideas and learn by doing.

Learning as a community

Children grow things together.
