Website information

Privacy & child safety.

This site shares information about the programme with families, partners and supporters. Here is what visitors can expect when using it.

Information on this website

The site has no child login, public chat, comments, sign-up form or online donation checkout. If you use the email or WhatsApp links, your message is handled by those services and their privacy practices.

The website is hosted by Render. Read Render’s privacy policy for information about its own services.

Photos and stories

Photos and stories help show the children’s creative work and activities. If you have a concern about a photo or story, or would like it reviewed, please contact us and identify the page or image. Please avoid sending sensitive information about a child in your message.

Safeguarding questions

This page covers the website only. It does not replace a full programme safeguarding or privacy policy. Parents and caregivers can contact us directly with questions about children’s participation, images, or the programme’s safeguarding arrangements.
