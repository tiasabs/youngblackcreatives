About Us

A creative home where children can make, belong and grow.

Young Black Creatives is an after-school creative programme in Langa, Cape Town. It offers children a safe, welcoming space to explore materials, express ideas and learn alongside one another.

Our purpose

We make room for children’s creativity to lead.

We don’t spark creativity. Creativity is our talent as Black people.

Through ceramics, gardening, drawing, painting, storytelling, theatre, song and dance, children can experiment with the materials and ideas around them. Some sessions have a shared focus; open studio time gives children space to choose what to do and discover where their ideas take them.

Led by artist and ceramicist Lungiswa Joe, the programme is built around trust, curiosity and care. Children are encouraged to speak up, solve problems, collaborate and find their own voices.

About the founder

Chantell Lungiswa Joe

The studio began with a simple invitation: children who were curious about clay could come in and try it for themselves.

When Lungiswa rented a classroom in a local school for her ceramics studio, a few local children started stopping by with questions. She invited them in to play with clay and gave them room to experiment. Word spread, and a small class grew.

Today, 15 children attend the programme formally four days a week. Some also drop in on Mondays and Tuesdays to see Lungiswa. They call her “Mama” and love having a space of their own.

That beginning still guides the studio. Children follow their curiosity, make things together and learn to care for clay, soil and the land. Lungiswa wants to give young people room to discover what they can do, and to bring visitors into Langa to see its beauty and what local people create.

Lungiswa is a ceramic artist, costume designer and curator. Her work draws on art, fashion, design, philosophy and heritage, which she brings into the studio while letting children shape their own discoveries.
