About Us

A creative home where children can make, belong and grow.

Young Black Creatives is an after-school creative programme in Langa, Cape Town. It offers children a safe, welcoming space to explore materials, express ideas and learn alongside one another.

Our purpose

We make room for children’s creativity to lead.

We don’t spark creativity. Creativity is our talent as Black people.

Through ceramics, gardening, drawing, painting, storytelling, theatre, song and dance, children can experiment with the materials and ideas around them. Some sessions have a shared focus; open studio time gives children space to choose what to do and discover where their ideas take them.

Led by artist and ceramicist Lungiswa Joe, the programme is built around trust, curiosity and care. Children are encouraged to speak up, solve problems, collaborate and find their own voices.

The ideas that guide us

Creativity. Stewardship. Storytelling.
