Our Partners

Community grows when we share what we know.

Artists, makers, gardeners, storytellers, organizations and local families can help children encounter new skills and possibilities.

Learning with others

Visits that open new doors.

Visiting artists and specialists share skills with the children. Trips introduce them to new places, people and ways of making.

Partnerships work best when they are grounded in trust, clear communication and a genuine exchange. A good partnership adds opportunities while keeping children’s creativity at the center.

Two ways to partner

Bring what you can share.

✳

As an individual

Share your time, knowledge or creative practice directly with the children.

  • Lead a hands-on session in art, ceramics, gardening, food or storytelling
  • Offer useful materials, tools or growing supplies
  • Help arrange a thoughtful visit or outing
  • Connect the studio with local people and places
✦

As a business or organisation

Build a partnership that brings resources and new experiences to the programme.

  • Sponsor ongoing costs, equipment or a specific project
  • Donate materials that match the studio’s current needs
  • Host a behind-the-scenes visit or practical learning day
  • Offer staff expertise or a longer-term collaboration
