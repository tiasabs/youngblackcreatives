Our Partners

Community grows when we share what we know.

Artists, makers, gardeners, storytellers, organizations and local families can help children encounter new skills and possibilities.

Learning with others

Visits that open new doors.

Visiting artists and specialists share skills with the children. Trips introduce them to new places, people and ways of making.

Partnerships work best when they are grounded in trust, clear communication and a genuine exchange. A good partnership adds opportunities while keeping children’s creativity at the center.

A visit behind the scenes

Honest Chocolate

The children ended their school term with a visit to the Honest Chocolate factory. They met the team, saw the craft up close and left with ideas about how chocolate is made.

Thank you to Aa'ishah and the team for welcoming the young creatives so warmly.

Share a skill or open a door

There are many ways to partner.

  • Offer a hands-on skill session in art, ceramics, gardening, food, film or storytelling.
  • Host a thoughtful outing or visit that helps children explore how things grow or are made.
  • Share useful tools, materials, growing space or community knowledge.
  • Help connect the programme with local people and places in Langa.
