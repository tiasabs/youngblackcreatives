Our Partners

Community grows when we share what we know.

Artists, makers, gardeners, storytellers, organizations and local families can help children encounter new skills and possibilities.

Learning with others

New skills. New places. More ways to imagine.

Visiting experts share specific skills with the children. Outings and visits open a window onto different materials, processes and places—from seeing how something is made to learning from people who work with their hands and ideas.

Partnerships work best when they are grounded in trust, clear communication and a genuine exchange. The goal is to add possibilities for children while keeping their creativity and voice at the center.

A visit behind the scenes

Honest Chocolate

A factory visit gave children a chance to see how chocolate is made and meet people working in a creative business. The collaboration connected their curiosity about materials and making with a local producer.

Story & screen

Stage 2 Screen

A film collaboration has helped connect children with storytelling and the process of making short films, bringing new creative skills into the studio.

Share a skill or open a door

There are many ways to partner.

  • Offer a hands-on skill session in art, ceramics, gardening, food, film or storytelling.
  • Host a thoughtful outing or visit that helps children explore how things grow or are made.
  • Share useful tools, materials, growing space or community knowledge.
  • Help connect the programme with local people and places in Langa.
