A few good ways to show up

Give what you can.

Money

Help cover studio essentials or equipment for the children’s work.

See examples
  • Rent and electricity
  • Clay and glaze
  • Art supplies and garden materials
  • Kilns and a plastic shredder

Materials

Share tools and supplies that children can use to make, grow and explore.

See current needs
  • Clay and ceramics: Terracotta or white stoneware clay, wooden modeling tools, bright primary underglazes, clear glaze and paintbrushes
  • Art supplies: Sketchbooks, charcoal, paint, pastels, crayons, watercolours, paper and brushes
  • For the garden: Child-sized cotton aprons, trowels, watering cans, vegetable, herb and flower seeds or seedlings, organic potting soil and compost
  • For gathering: Woven harvest baskets and mats for the storytelling circle

Time and skills

Share a practice, help with a garden session or open a door to a new place.

See examples
  • Offer a hands-on workshop
  • Help with gardening, ceramics, art or storytelling
  • Support an outing or learning visit
  • Connect the studio with local people and places
