Support Us

Help make more room for children to create.

Young Black Creatives needs money, useful resources and caring people with time to share. Every contribution helps sustain a safe, hands-on creative space in Langa.

Find out how you can contribute

Three ways to be part of it

Give what helps the work grow.

Contribute money

Financial support helps cover the regular costs of keeping the programme going, as well as larger equipment needs.

  • Studio rent and electricity
  • Clay, glaze and ceramics materials
  • Art supplies, paper and paint
  • Seeds, plants and growing materials
  • Equipment such as kilns and a plastic shredder

Share resources

Useful tools and materials give children more ways to experiment, build and care for their space.

  • Natural clay, underglazes and clear glaze
  • Modeling tools and child-sized aprons
  • Sketchbooks, charcoal, paint, pastels and crayons
  • Trowels, watering cans, seeds, soil and compost
  • Harvest baskets and mats for storytelling

Give time & skills

People who enjoy sharing what they know can help children encounter new ideas and experiences.

  • Offer a specific skill session
  • Support gardening, ceramics, art or storytelling
  • Help with an outing or educational visit
  • Connect the programme with local spaces and people

Please check with the team before dropping off materials or planning a visit. That way, contributions can match the children’s current projects and the studio’s needs.
