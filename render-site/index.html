What makes this programme distinctive

Children help shape what happens here.

Young Black Creatives combines shared creative sessions with open studio time. Children choose, experiment and collaborate; adults create a safe, welcoming space and offer materials, boundaries and support.

It is a place to belong as well as a place to learn: children return to explore ideas over time, build relationships and grow their confidence through hands-on discovery.

Color, purpose & community · Mandela Day

Honoring the legacy. Painting the future.

Young Black Creatives spent Mandela Day putting art where it belongs: in the heart of the community, for everyone to share at Hamilton Nake.

Children brought color and care to a shared public space—celebrating community and imagining what we can make together.
