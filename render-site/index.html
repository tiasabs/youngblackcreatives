A safe space to make, belong & grow.

Young Black Creatives is a programme for children and teens to make with clay, grow food, tell stories and explore ideas in a welcoming space.

How children spend time here

Make, grow and share stories.

Make

Clay, drawing and painting give children room to test ideas and learn through their hands.

Grow

In the garden, children plant, tend and harvest while learning to care for living things.

Share

Stories, song and group projects help children listen, collaborate and express themselves.

A child-led approach

Room to try things their own way.

An open studio can begin with “I’m bored” and end with a shared invention. Children pick up materials, trade ideas and keep trying until something takes shape.
